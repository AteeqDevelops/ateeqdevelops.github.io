01Data Collection & Usage

PeerDrop is an offline-first, peer-to-peer (P2P) utility that lets you send and receive files directly between nearby Android devices. It works without an account, sign-in, or internet connection.

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your personal data or your files to any cloud server.

Files you choose to share go only to the device you select. Ateeq Develops has no access to the files you transfer, their names, or any information about who you share with.

Information visible to nearby devices. To make a transfer work, your device name is visible to nearby devices during discovery, and the name and size of a file are shared with the receiving device. This exchange happens only between the devices involved and is never sent to us.

Retention and your control. PeerDrop keeps no copies of your files. Files you receive are saved on your own device, where you can view or delete them at any time using your file manager. Uninstalling the app removes any data the app stored locally.

02App Permissions

PeerDrop requests only the permissions needed to discover nearby devices and complete local transfers. Depending on your Android version, these may include:

  • Nearby Devices / Storage. Used to find and connect to devices close to you, to read the files you select to send, and to save the files you receive. The app accesses only the files you explicitly choose.
  • Location (Wi-Fi Direct discovery). Android requires this permission for apps to scan for nearby Wi-Fi Direct peers. PeerDrop uses it solely for device discovery. Your location is not tracked, recorded, stored, or shared.
  • Wi-Fi / Network State. Used to check whether Wi-Fi is enabled and to establish and manage the direct local connection between devices.

You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device Settings. Some features may not work if required permissions are denied.

03Third-Party Services & Analytics

PeerDrop does not include any tracking or advertising SDKs that harvest personal data. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers, and we do not build user profiles.

Please note that Google Play and your Android device may independently collect standard diagnostic information, such as crash reports, under Google's own privacy policy. This is outside the control of PeerDrop and Ateeq Develops.

04Security

Files transfer directly between devices using local network protocols such as Wi-Fi Direct and your local wireless network. Your data does not pass through any external server or the internet during a transfer, which greatly reduces the risk of interception by remote parties.

We recommend sharing only with people and devices you trust, and reviewing any incoming transfer request before accepting it.

05Children's Privacy

PeerDrop does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, there is nothing to collect from or share about a child. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will look into it promptly.
